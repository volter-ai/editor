# Blind walk runsheet: from discovery to Cyclotron, by persona (walk 6)

You are a stranger who has just heard of videogame.ai. You know only what the screens show you. Start each persona
in a fresh browser profile or a private window, on the device named, and take the first link a person like you
would take. Write PASS or FAIL beside each step with what you saw; stop a step at its first FAIL, copy the exact
text on screen and save a screenshot. Do not read any source, do not fix anything, run no tests, and never start
a ChatGPT or Claude sign-in or submit any form: a step that reaches one stops there and says so.

**Look, don't read the markup.** Judge every web page and the editor from screenshots, as a person sees them: take one,
look at it, act (click, scroll, type), take the next. Never read a page through its source, its accessibility tree,
innerText or a fetch. A wall of text, or the same thing said twice, is a finding, never a pass.

Each persona starts at **www.videogame.ai** unless its row says otherwise, and ends at the step named in "Done".

## Steps (every persona)

1. **Arrive.** Open the start page. PASS: from the first screenshot, at a glance, you can tell what the company does and
   see the way to a tool you can use today.
2. **Find Cyclotron.** Take the link a person like you would take. PASS: you reach cyclotron.videogame.ai in at most
   two clicks, without searching.
3. **See it.** Scroll Cyclotron's page a screen at a time. PASS: the pictures have shown you what Cyclotron does before
   it asks you to install, and the button to start is simple and obvious. FAIL: a screen that is mostly text.
4. **Reach "Done"** for your row. PASS: as the row says, with no dead link, no contradiction between pages, and no
   claim you could not act on.
5. **Start it** (on a computer). Open the browser version from Cyclotron's page in Chrome or Edge and wait for the
   first model, or, where your persona would install, have the install line for your computer open and copied (do
   not run it here; walks 1 to 4 do). PASS: the first model appears, or the line is in hand with what it needs.
   The phone persona is N/A here.
