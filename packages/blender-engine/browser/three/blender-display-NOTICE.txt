Blender display processors
==========================

blender-display-shaders.json and blender-display-shaders.lut are generated
using OpenColorIO 2.5.0 from Blender's color-management configuration.
The pinned configuration is publicly available at:
https://github.com/volter-ai/blender/blob/071e080a21c09e07f2dac8593dca2f9a566e591e/release/datafiles/colormanagement/config.ocio

Configuration SHA-256:
47a7d83e79c1d21f49ba6c505efe311da723471688c614b5c366e1da7eb8ea3a

Filmic configuration: Troy James Sobotka, with the contributors named in
config.ocio. AgX: based on Troy Sobotka's AgX, further developed by Zijun Eary
Zhou, Mark Faderbauer and Sakari Kapanen. The generated operations and table
payload preserve the source configuration; Blender source/license information
is available at that repository's release/license/ directory.
