# Development Log

This is the project's internal development journal. It records the reasoning
that source history and playtest receipts cannot: what changed, why that path
was chosen, what was learned, and what remains.

Add one entry when a coherent workstream or feature arc finishes—not after
every small edit. Put the newest entry first and cover:

- what changed and why;
- important decisions, rejected alternatives, and lessons;
- the playtest outcome and anything still unresolved;
- links to the best representative screenshots and, when motion matters, a
  short gameplay clip.

Evidence linked here is durable project material. Review the actual candidate
captures, reject unclear or unrepresentative frames, and watch each selected
clip from beginning to end. Copy only the chosen media out of the bounded
`.vgai/` run history into `media/screenshots/` or `media/clips/` before linking
it here. Never cite an unreviewed capture merely because it is the latest one.
